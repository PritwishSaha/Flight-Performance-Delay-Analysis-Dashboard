## 📌 Project Overview

The **Flight Performance & Delay Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence project** designed to analyze flight operations, airline performance, delays, and operational trends.

The raw flight dataset was cleaned and prepared using **Microsoft Excel** before being transformed into an interactive dashboard in **Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a clear view of:

* ✈️ Total flight operations
* 🏢 Airline-wise flight volume
* ⏱️ Departure and arrival delays
* 📏 Average flight distance
* 📊 Airline market share
* 📅 Daily flight trends
* 🎯 Overall operational performance

The goal is to convert raw flight data into **actionable business insights** for understanding airline operations and performance.

# 🔍 Key Insights

### ✈️ Flight Operations

The dataset contains approximately **134K flights**, providing a substantial base for analyzing airline operations.

### 🏆 Airline Market Share

A small group of airlines — **AA, DL, UA, WN, and AS** — account for a large proportion of the total flight volume.

### 📅 Daily Flight Patterns

Daily flight volume shows recurring peaks and dips, suggesting potential **weekly or seasonal operational patterns**.

### ⏱️ Delay Performance

The calculated average departure delay is approximately **-0.56 minutes**, while the average arrival delay is approximately **-7.43 minutes**.

Because the averages are negative, the correct interpretation is that flights were **slightly earlier than their scheduled times on average** in this dataset.

### 📏 Flight Distance

The average flight distance is approximately **727.62 miles**, providing an overview of the typical route length in the dataset.

> **Note:** Negative delay values generally indicate that a flight arrived or departed earlier than scheduled. They should not automatically be interpreted as "zero delay."
